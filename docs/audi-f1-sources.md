## F. How the figures flow into the product

- `public/js/customer.js` is the single source of truth. Each entry carries `source: "A3"`-style keys
  into this file or an `assumption: "..."` string. `public/js/catalog.js`, `briefs.js`, `model.js`,
  `scene.js`, `race.js`, `case.js` and the README derive their numbers from it.
- `public/case.html` recomputes the business case live from the same module (formulae are listed at
  the top of `public/js/case.js`).
- Nothing in the product claims knowledge of the customer's actual contracts, vendors or room
  design; every such value is labelled "planning assumption" on screen.
