Where should Audi F1's 2027 IT estate live?

Three paths for the same hardware: keep the Hinwil data room as it is, retrofit it, or commit 500 kW at Digital Realty's Zurich campus (Glattbrugg/Rümlang, 30 km away) with a contractual right to grow for 2028. Cash, cost-cap charge, electricity, CO2, resilience and 2028 headroom, side by side. IT hardware is identical in every path and left out here; the infrastructure performance simulator models it, with HPE via HPE GreenLake as the working assumption for the OEM.

How the numbers are built

  • Facility electricity = IT load served × 0.85 load factor × 8,760 h × PUE. Electricity cost = kWh × the site's all-in tariff (sliders, Rp/kWh).
  • Digital Realty fee = CHF per kW per month × committed kW (500 kW in 2027; the 2028 load from 2028 under the expansion right) × 12. Power is metered on top.
  • Yearly cost-cap charge = electricity + fee + one-off plant ÷ 10 years (straight-line, zero residual, FIA Financial Regulations D6.1.1.k). Conservative: the fee, fit-out and plant are all treated as in-cap; see the treatment row for what the auditors may exclude.
  • 3-year TCO (2027–2029, cash) = one-off 2027 + one-off 2028 + yearly cash 2027 + 2 × yearly cash 2028. A Hinwil path that cannot carry the 2028 load is charged a new hall at CHF 12,000 per IT kW for the shortfall [D8]; the campus path is charged its expansion fit-out.
  • CO2 = facility kWh × 75 g CO2eq/kWh, the Swiss location-based consumer mix midpoint of 2024 and 2025 [D4, D-c]. A certified renewable product lowers it; the comparison between sites does not change.
  • Cost cap in CHF: USD 215M is CHF 190.0M at the FIA's fixed 1.1316 USD/CHF [C1, C2], CHF 180.6M at the 0.84 planning rate [E-a]. Shares of the cap use the planning rate.
