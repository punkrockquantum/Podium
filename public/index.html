Pick your table

Four tables, one customer problem each. Tap the table you are sitting at. Nothing to set up: the game is open.

How to play

  1. Add your team: type a name, tap once. At every other table, tap your team in the list.
  2. Brief: read it together. It holds every number you need.
  3. Tune: three dials. Each choice locks the moment you tap it, so decide first.
  4. Pitch: Call the Solutions Architect. Approved tables find seconds per lap for the race.
