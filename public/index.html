FIA 2027 Infrastructure Specification · Issue 01

Build the infrastructure that wins the finale.

Race against the game clock: teams move freely between the tables; on each table's iPad, choose your team first. Every table works the same way (about 10 minutes each): 1 Brief, then Start designing; 2 Decide, four to six decisions; 3 Simulate, Run simulation; 4 Pitch, Lock in (take a screenshot), then Call the leader and pitch. At zero every table locks, and any table you did not lock in races on its default options. The finale is the Austin, Texas 2027 Grand Prix at the Circuit of the Americas.

On this Mac, open the host console, the projector, or a table below. The host PIN is the one printed in the terminal (2027 unless you set HOST_PIN).
